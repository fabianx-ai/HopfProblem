## Findings

1. `[docstring]` `Lib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Leray/FibreStalkEvaluation/Neighborhood.lean:165` (commit `cfe24fa9`), `neighborhoodCohomologyEquiv_symm_apply`. Docstring added: "The inverse of `neighborhoodCohomologyEquiv` is inverse to the forward comparison `Hⁿ(T, G) → H'ⁿ(U, i_*G)`." Statement: `(neighborhoodCohomologyEquiv i U hU hi hfinite G n).symm a = neighborhoodCohomologyForward i U hU hi hfinite G n a := rfl` — i.e. the inverse of the equivalence *is* the forward comparison (the equivalence is `(Equiv.ofBijective forward _).symm`), not "inverse to" it. Confirmed at head `39f1d12b` by `#check` (scratch file, see Tool notes). Should read "The inverse of `neighborhoodCohomologyEquiv` is the forward comparison `Hⁿ(T,G) → H'ⁿ(U, i_*G)`".

2. `[citation]` Lettered sub-references to Weibel Theorem 2.4.6 — "2.4.6(a)" (`Lib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/RightDerived.lean`, commit `8056133a`, module docstring and `rightDerived_additive`), "2.4.6(b)" (`RightDerived/Connecting.lean` `6c3cbc75`; `CohomologicalDeltaFunctor/RightDerived.lean` `a2ad1935`), "2.4.6(c)" (`CohomologicalDeltaFunctor/RightDerived.lean`, effaceability and universality docstrings). As I recall Weibel, Theorem 2.4.6 is a single statement ("if A has enough injectives and F is left exact, the R^iF form a universal δ-functor") with no lettered parts; the additivity / long-exact-sequence / vanishing-on-injectives items live in 2.4.1–2.4.5 and Exercise 2.4.x. I am not certain of the lettering, but the packet uses (a), (b), (c) for three different facts as if the theorem were itemised, and "Exercise 2.4.3" is cited for "a natural isomorphism F ≅ G induces Rⁿ F ≅ Rⁿ G", which I cannot place. Someone with the book should confirm or strip the letters and the exercise number. Note also that the Lean statements in `CohomologicalDeltaFunctor/RightDerived.lean` require only `[F.Additive]`, not left exactness; the docstrings say so honestly, but then the cited theorem is only "see also".

## Tool notes

* The receipt's replacement for the missing envdiff (source-level "nothing outside a docstring changed") was easy to reproduce and is in fact the stronger statement for *this* branch; but the merged-head envdiff attributes 76 changed-type names to this packet's modules, 63 of which this packet did not cause. A per-packet envdiff would have shown 13; the merged one cannot separate own changes from induced ones. The envdiff tool's "PROOF-NAMING" class (uses-set unchanged) is what makes universe lifts pass, and it also makes *any* type change that keeps the same constant set pass — it would not catch a hypothesis being strengthened from `[T1Space Y]` to `[T2Space Y]`, for example. A hash of the type with universe levels erased would be a more honest "universe-only" detector.
* `dump_base.jsonl` / `dump_after.jsonl` with `typeHash` vs `typeHashPublic` per name were more useful than `envdiff.json`; a receipt template that lists, per changed name, "old header → new header" (as this receipt does for its 13) is the right format.
* The binder counter should be defined in the receipt (occurrences vs declarations); as written the totals cannot be checked.
