## Tool notes

* Packet receipts should commit their `envdiff.json` (the preamble/names branches did); without it the central envdiff claim of a packet is unverifiable except through the merged file.
* Receipt summaries should be generated from the per-file tables, not typed: three of four headline numbers here disagree with the receipt's own tables, and the wrong numbers propagated into the commit message and the coordinator's brief.
* The round-8 pins receipt names `TopCat.FiniteClosedPushforward.pushforwardStalkEquiv` as a chokepoint "in `FiniteClosedPushforward/Exact.lean`"; that declaration lives in `FiniteClosedPushforward.lean` and was lifted by this packet — `Exact.lean`'s pin was its own `variable {X Y : TopCat.{0}}`. Worth fixing in the round-8 receipt so the two receipts agree.
* A "forced by" claim should name the forcer that actually binds after the named one is lifted; a one-line `grep` of the file's imports for `Type`-pinned interfaces (`SingularCochains`) would have caught Finding 1.
