-- (and `_at_union`, `_at_inter`): … → H^q(X, A ∪ B) → H^q((C(X)/C(A) ⊕ C(X)/C(B))^∨) → H^q(X, A ∩ B) → H^{q+1}(X, A ∪ B) → …

### Design notes

* Cohomology is `Lib`'s `dualComplex (AddCommGrpCat.of ℤ)` (the same cochains as the center seats
  and as `cochainRestriction_homologyMap_isIso`), so no new cochain framework was introduced; the
  chain-level cochains `Cochain X k` of M3 are its degreewise `cochainLinear`.
* Excision in cohomology does not dualize the homological excision quasi-isomorphism (the dual of a
  quasi-isomorphism of free complexes needs the UCT): the middle map `C^*(X) → C^*(C(U) + C(V))` is
  a quasi-isomorphism by the cochain form of the small simplices theorem (dual of a chain homotopy
  equivalence), and `quasiIso_τ₁` transports it to the relative term. This is the route recorded in
  PLAN §6 (textbook N02/N04).
* The relative Mayer–Vietoris sequence is stated with the cohomology of the dual of the biproduct
  `C(X)/C(A) ⊕ C(X)/C(B)` in the middle; its identification with `H^q(X, A) × H^q(X, B)` (additivity
  of the dual and of cohomology) is routine and not written.
