```lean
theorem RelativeSingularCohomology.cohomology_excision_bijective (U V : Set X) (hU : IsOpen U) (hV : IsOpen V)

-- H^q(X, U) → H^q(V, V ∩ U) is an isomorphism

theorem RelativeSingularCohomology.cohomology_exact_at_space (A : Set X) (q : ℕ) :
    AddMonoidHom.range (cohomologyProjection A q) = AddMonoidHom.ker (cohomologySubspace A q)
-- (and `_at_relative`, `_at_subspace`): … → H^q(X, A) → H^q(X) → H^q(A) → H^{q+1}(X, A) → …

theorem RelativeSingularCohomology.cohomologyMV_exact_at_pair (A B : Set X) (hA : IsOpen A) (hB : IsOpen B) (q : ℕ) :
    AddMonoidHom.range (cohomologyMVLeft A B hA hB q) = AddMonoidHom.ker (cohomologyMVRight A B q)
-- (and `_at_union`, `_at_inter`): … → H^q(X, A ∪ B) → H^q((C(X)/C(A) ⊕ C(X)/C(B))^∨) → H^q(X, A ∩ B) → H^{q+1}(X, A ∪ B) → …
```

### Design notes

* Cohomology is `Lib`'s `dualComplex (AddCommGrpCat.of ℤ)` (the same cochains as the center seats
  and as `cochainRestriction_homologyMap_isIso`), so no new cochain framework was introduced; the
  chain-level cochains `Cochain X k` of M3 are its degreewise `cochainLinear`.
* Excision in cohomology does not dualize the homological excision quasi-isomorphism (the dual of a
  quasi-isomorphism of free complexes needs the UCT): the middle map `C^*(X) → C^*(C(U) + C(V))` is
  a quasi-isomorphism by the cochain form of the small simplices theorem (dual of a chain homotopy
  equivalence), and `quasiIso_τ₁` transports it to the relative term. This is the route recorded in
  PLAN §6 (textbook N02/N04).
* The relative Mayer–Vietoris sequence is stated with the cohomology of the dual of the biproduct
  `C(X)/C(A) ⊕ C(X)/C(B)` in the middle; its identification with `H^q(X, A) × H^q(X, B)` (additivity
  of the dual and of cohomology) is routine and not written.

### Design notes

* The cap product is descended twice: `homologyDesc` (M0's cycle API) in the homology variable
  for a fixed cocycle, then `cohomologyDescSucc`/`cohomologyDescZero` (new cocycle API) in the
  cohomology variable. Both well-definedness proofs are the Leibniz formula of M3 with one of the
  two terms killed (`∂c ⌢ ψ = 0` for `∂c ∈ C(A)`, `c ⌢ δψ = 0` for a cocycle); degree `0` needs
  no coboundary argument (`cohomologyDescZero`).
* The degrees are kept as `k l m` with `h : k + l = m` so that the two Leibniz instances
  (`c ∈ C_{k+l+1}` with `(k, l+1)` and with `(k+1, l)`) can be reached by `subst`.
* The base case avoids a general "Hom from a cyclic group" argument: `H_{m+2}(E, E ∖ K) ≅ ℤ` sends
  `μ_K` to a unit (its span is everything), and `evalAt_bijective` is proved by hand.
* Instance pitfalls recorded: `Module ℤ (ModuleHomology.Cycle (relativeComplex X A) m)` is not
  found through the quotient-complex abbreviation (`relativeCycleModule` pins it); the two cycle
  APIs (`ModuleHomology`, `ChainCycleLift`) and the two cocycle APIs (`CochainCocycle`,
  `PositiveCocycle`) are definitionally the same and are bridged by `change`.
