## Design notes (deviations from the plan, and why)

* Three `ℤ`-module instances are pinned in `ChainSubmodule` (`carrierModule`, `quotientModule`,
  `quotientCarrierModule`) because `AddCommGroup.toIntModule` and `Submodule.module` are not
  definitionally equal (checked: `rfl` fails) — the diamond Lib already pins in `smallChainModule`.
  `subComplex`/`quotientComplex`/`relativeComplex` are `abbrev`s so that instance search sees
  through `.X n`.
* `trace U V : Set V := Subtype.val ⁻¹' U` is a `def`: with the preimage written inline, the two
  elaborations of `(↑) ⁻¹' U` in the definition and in its use were not syntactically equal and the
  unifier spent >200k heartbeats comparing generating sets of `supportedChainSubmodule`.
* The `MapsTo` arguments of `relativeHomologyMap` are left arbitrary in the `_id`/`_comp` lemmas
  (`relativeHomologyMap_congr` for equal maps), since `mapsTo_id A : MapsTo id A A` is not
  type-correct at implicit transparency for `MapsTo ⇑(ContinuousMap.id X) A A`.
