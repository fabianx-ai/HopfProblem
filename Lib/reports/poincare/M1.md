## Design notes

* Compact homology balls (`IsHomologyBall`: the local restriction `H_n(M, M ∖ K) → H_n(M, M ∖ {x})`
  is bijective at every `x ∈ K`) replace Hatcher's open balls, because the open-cover excision of
  M0 needs `M ∖ K` open; this is also the form of the accepted textbook (P11, compact coordinate
  balls, sections over the interior).
* The manifold enters only through `exists_isHomologyBall`; every later statement (covering space,
  orientations, M2) uses homology balls, so the dimension hypothesis `2 ≤ n` appears only where `ℤ`
  is needed (`localHomology_equiv_int`, hence `isOrientable_of_simplyConnectedSpace`).
* Three elaboration lessons recorded for the next files: a `local notation` that mentions a
  section variable breaks inside tactic blocks ("unknown free variable"); `by` blocks inside
  `fun` binders of a `where` structure field do the same — state the proof as a lemma; the fibre
  type of the trivialization is a `def` (`Fibre`) so that its discrete topology does not leak
  onto `RelativeHomology`, with all rewriting routed through `trivInvFun`.

## Open items (M1)

* Dimensions `n = 0, 1`: `localHomologyEuclideanEquiv` and `localHomology_equiv_int` are stated for
  `n ≥ 2` (the sphere computation of `Lib` starts at `S¹`, and `n = 1` needs `H₀` of two points).
  The orientation theory (`Orientation`, the covering space) is stated for all `n`.
* The orientation *double* cover (generators only) is not built as a space; the lifting argument
  uses the `ℤ`-cover and the clopen generator set instead, which is all "simply connected ⇒
  orientable" needs.
