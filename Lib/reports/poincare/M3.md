## Statements of the main results

```lean
theorem SingularCapProduct.boundary_capChain (k l : ℕ) (φ : Cochain X k) (c : Chains X (k + l + 1)) :
    ((singularComplex X).d (l + 1) l).hom (capChain k (l + 1) (k + l + 1) rfl φ c) =
      (-1 : ℤ) ^ k • (capChain k l (k + l) rfl φ (((singularComplex X).d (k + l + 1) (k + l)).hom c)
        - capChain (k + 1) l (k + l + 1) _ (coboundary k φ) c)

theorem SingularCapProduct.coboundary_cupChain (k l : ℕ) (φ : Cochain X k) (ψ : Cochain X l) :
    coboundary (k + l) (cupChain k l (k + l) rfl φ ψ) =
      cupChain (k + 1) l (k + l + 1) _ (coboundary k φ) ψ + (-1 : ℤ) ^ k • cupChain k (l + 1) (k + l + 1) rfl φ (coboundary l ψ)

theorem SingularCapProduct.inducedChain_capChain (f : C(X, Y)) (k l m) (h : k + l = m) (φ : Cochain Y k) (c) :
    inducedChain f l (capChain k l m h (pullback f k φ) c) = capChain k l m h φ (inducedChain f m c)
theorem SingularCapProduct.pullback_cupChain (f : C(X, Y)) (k l m) (h) (φ ψ) :
    pullback f m (cupChain k l m h φ ψ) = cupChain k l m h (pullback f k φ) (pullback f l ψ)
```

## Design notes

* The degrees are carried as `k l m` with a proof `h : k + l = m` instead of `k + l` in the types,
  so that the Leibniz formula can be stated with both `k + l + 1` and `(k + 1) + l` without casts.
* Cochains are the plain `ℤ`-linear duals `Chains X k →ₗ[ℤ] ℤ`; the bridge to `Lib`'s
  `dualComplex (AddCommGrpCat.of ℤ)` cochains (M4a) is `cochainAddHom`/`LinearMap.toAddMonoidHom`
  degreewise and is left to the homology-level descent.

## Not done in M3 (carried to M4b/M4c)

  `H^k(X, A) ⊗ H_{k+l}(X, A) → H_l(X)` and `H^k(X) ⊗ H_{k+l}(X, A) → H_l(X, A)` (the Leibniz formula
  is the whole content; the quotient bookkeeping is routine but not written).
* The pairing identity `φ ⌢ α = ⟨φ, α⟩ · [pt]` in degree `k = l` at the homology level
  (`capSimplex_top` is its chain form).
