theorem ManifoldOrientation.LocalToGlobal.of_isCompact [ChartedSpace (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)) M] [T2Space M]

The last statement is consumer (c) in generic form: a closed simply connected manifold `X` modelled
on `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 6)` has `H₆(X; ℤ) ≅ ℤ` (`hn : 2 ≤ 6`), with `[X]` characterized by its
local restrictions. What the center seat must supply (their CHARGED N34): `CompactSpace X`,
`T2Space X`, the `ChartedSpace (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 6)) X` instance of its smooth structure, and
`SimplyConnectedSpace X`.

## Design notes

* The property is indexed by the open set `U = M ∖ A` so that the relative Mayer–Vietoris sequence
  of M0 (`relativeLeftMap U V`, …) applies with no rewriting of complements; the compact sets enter
  only through `LocalToGlobal.congr` at the end.
* Hatcher's step (2) ("the complement of a compact convex set retracts onto a sphere") is the
  convex case of `IsHomologyBall` from M1, transported into the manifold as *chart-convex sets*;
  the whole development stays in `M`, no family of local classes is ever transported to `ℝⁿ`.
* Hatcher's cubes are closed balls; the compactness step uses only that the *boundary* support of
  a representative misses the balls (`boundary_mem_supported_compl_of_disjoint`).
* Surjectivity of `H_n(M) → H_n(M, M ∖ {x})` is proved from the fundamental class generating the
  target, avoiding the `ℤ`-smul diamond (`AddCommGroup.toIntModule` vs the module smul of the
  homology objects) that a "multiples of the orientation" argument runs into.
